Owner report on v11: a card shows beside its icon for a moment, then jumps far away (Color ended at the far
left beside the toolbox; Color Guide / Pattern Options mid-canvas; Swatches partly under the tab bar); the

Preferences switch "Panel cards (Varos)" to compare with upstream's own panel system.

WHAT THE OWNER WILL SEE
- An icon of the column opens its card once, in its place: no flyout flashing beside the icon first, no
  jump. Right edge 6 pt left of the dock, top level with its icon (never under the document tabs, never
  over the status bars), stacking down under the cards already there, cascading (24 pt steps) only when the
  column is full (e.g. Color + Swatches + Color Guide + Pattern Options in a 900 pt high window).
- On the first start of this build, card places saved by v10/v11 are forgotten once (they were measured
  against the old dock and were why Color opened at x 116 and Color Guide mid-canvas); the open cards are
  placed again beside their icons. Tab groups (e.g. Character + Paragraph) are kept. After that, a card
  moved by hand and closed comes back where it was left, as in v10.
- Every card is fully opaque from its first frame (no fade-in, no ghost).
- A lit icon (its card open) is the kit's toggle: dark well, 1 pt line, and now a bright glyph (before:
  dim glyph in the well).
- Text fields: the value text is centred again and every field is as tall as in v10 (Opacity, Stroke,
  X/Y/W/H, ...).
- The Control bar's "Snapping" is a link label like "Stroke:" / "Opacity:" / "Style:" (no framed
  button); a click opens the same menu.
- Preferences › User Interface, right under Auto-Collapse Iconic Panels: "Panel cards (Varos)", on by
  default. Off: icons behave exactly as upstream (one flyout at a time, the group's tabs, drag an icon out
  to float it as upstream's floating panel / OS window); open cards close and the newest shows as its
  flyout. On again: that flyout becomes a card. The docking layout is never changed by the switch. Saved
  in ui.json as varos_panel_stack.enabled.

ROOT CAUSES
1. "Beside its icon for a moment": craft-ui opens its own flyout in the frame of the icon click (before
   its FlyoutChanged event reaches Varos) and draws the group's FRONT panel in it (Swatches when Pattern
   Options was clicked) beside the icon. Varos then closed it and showed the card from the next frame.
   In debug builds the panel drawn twice in one frame (flyout + card) even raised egui's
   "Widget swatch-tile changed layer_id" assert (visible as "Internal error" in cards4-before.png).
2. "Then jumps far away": the card opened at its remembered place. Those places (ui.json
   varos_panel_stack.places) were saved by v10 against the old dock (Color at x 116, Color Guide at
   834,607) and were honoured as they were. Also: a card whose egui area was already known was drawn at
   its first guess (360 pt tall) and then re-placed by fit() the next frame (a second, smaller jump), and
   fit() placed cards against the others' not-yet-fitted sizes in the same frame.
3. "Under the tab bar" / over the status bars: since the merge the room was the whole window (the v10
   clamp to the icon column's bottom was lost and there was never a top limit under the document tabs).
4. Faded Swatches: the card's egui Area used egui's default fade-in (0.08 s); a card re-shown (reopened,
   or after the frame Varos skipped it for the flyout) was painted at ~20 % alpha in its first frames
   (measured: fill #05060732 instead of #1A1D23FF in the reopen frame). The cards also shared their
   area id with craft-ui's floating windows (area.with(("floating", first))), so their area state could
   mix with an upstream floating window of the same panel.
5. Field look: egui 0.37's default text-edit style gives every text edit at least the interact height
   (24 pt in our theme) with its text at the top (egui 0.36: as tall as the text). Our framed fields grew
   (24 + margins) or kept their 26 pt box with the text riding ~4 pt high.
6. "Snapping": upstream's new Control bar quick menu was an egui menu_button (framed default button).
7. Opacity field focused without a click: NOT reproduced. Tested: a dialog opening over the Control bar
   (its "focus the first field" flag), Tab / Shift+Tab twice, Escape, V, Cmd+Z, Cmd+A, clicks on
   "Opacity:" and "Style:" — no field takes the keyboard in any of them (now regression tests). Clicking
   the label right before a numeric field (upstream's scrub labels) focuses that field with its text
   selected by design; that may be what the owner hit. Left for the owner's hand test.

LEFT / RISKS
- Opacity-field focus not reproduced headlessly (see root cause 7).
- The egui "experimental" feature is enabled only to reach the widget-theme API (replace_widget_theme,
  StyleProvider); it is exported API of the pinned storytold egui rev, but egui marks it unstable: an
  egui bump may need this 15-line theme adjusted.
- A card Varos places appears 2 frames (~16-33 ms) after the click (measured unseen first); a card
  reopened at its saved place shows in the click frame.
- With Panel cards off, upstream itself starts no icon drag while the Character group's flyout is open
  (same with keep_open off before this lane); not changed.
- In a 900 pt high window the four cards of the report don't fit down the column: Color Guide and
  Pattern Options cascade over Color (v10 behaviour).
- cap/ (untracked, cap/panels3-cards.png, 21:45) is not from this lane; left as is.
- Not hand-tested in the real window.
