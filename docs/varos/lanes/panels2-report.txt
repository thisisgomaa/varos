Owner feedback on v8: several open panels were drawn inside ONE scrolling column beside the icon
rail ("a fake side list you scroll in"). He wants Illustrator: each panel an independent card.

BEHAVIOUR (keep-open mode, on by default)
- A click on an icon of the right icon column opens that panel as its own card: upstream's
  floating panel group (window.panel.float), placed beside the column and kept open. Each card
  has its own position and size, moves by its tab or the grip at the left of its tab strip,
  resizes by its bottom-right corner, and closes on its own (its x, its icon again, or
  Window > that panel, or its key: Cmd+T, F6... toggle their card as in Illustrator). An agent's
  plain window.panel on a card already open only shows it, never closes it (review fix 1).
- The scroll column is gone. No shared container: N panels = N floating groups.
- The icon stays in the column and stays lit while its card is open (cards are kept in the
  column even though they float; a panel DRAGGED out of the column still leaves it, as upstream
  and as Illustrator do).
- Docking and grouping are upstream's own: drop a card on another card's tabs and it becomes a
  tab of that card (each icon still closes its own tab); drop it on the left edge of the dock and
  it docks as a dock column; drop it on the icon column and it closes (it goes back to its icon
  instead of popping out of it).
- A card fits its panel: as tall as the contents (capped by the window, the rest scrolls), and
  wider (leftwards, so it never covers the icon column) only when a row of the panel doesn't fit
  the 256 pt default. Once the user resizes a card by hand, it keeps that size.
- A card closed and opened again comes back where it was left, at its size (per panel,
  ui.json: UiState.varos_panel_stack.places), unless half of it or more would lie on another
  card (then it takes a new free place), and always left of the icon column and the dock. When
  the window or the dock changes size (and on the first frame, so cards restored from ui.json
  too) cards reaching over the dock move back left of it, keeping their size. The open cards themselves survive a restart (they
  are upstream floating groups in ui.json; varos_panel_stack.cards remembers which ones are
  cards).
- Tab / Shift+Tab hide every card with the rest of the panels and bring them all back where they
  were (upstream hides floating groups with the dock).
- Panels opened another way (a Properties button such as Align, a workspace that opens a panel,
  F-key shortcuts) open as cards too. UiState.open_panel names the newest card, so upstream
  code/agents that read "the open panel" keep working.
- Unchanged upstream pop-outs: the tabbed group's panels (Properties/Layers/Libraries) in a
  collapsed dock still pop out one at a time; a panel dragged out of the column and dropped back
  pops out of its icon once. keep_open=false or Preferences > User Interface > Auto-Collapse
  Iconic Panels = upstream's one-pop-out-at-a-time behaviour (no cards).

PLACEMENT ALGORITHM (panel_stack::place, pure, unit-tested)
- New card size: remembered size, else 256 x 360 (then fitted to its contents next frame);
  height capped to the window.
- Remembered place exists -> that place, moved into the window if the window got smaller.
- Remembered place lying >= 50 % on another card (overlap area / smaller card's area) -> ignored.
- Everything is clamped into the "room": the window left of min(icon column left, docked columns
  left, dock rect left) - 6 pt.
- Else: right edge 6 pt left of the dock (left of the icon column, docked columns and dock rect),
  top aligned with the icon column's top. Walk down: while the candidate overlaps a floating
  group, jump to just below it (+6 pt). If it fits above the window bottom (8 pt margin) -> there.
  A card taller than the window still opens at the first slot.
- Column full -> cascade from the column's top: step k = (x - 24k, top + 24k), the first step
  whose corner isn't within 12 pt of an existing card's corner; clamped on screen.
- Existing card rects = the shared-layout windows' own rects (clamped like craft_ui draws them);
  native sets use their last drawn bounds; docked columns are ignored.

LEFT / RISKS
- Look: the cards are upstream's shared floating windows: square corners with a 1 pt border and
  craft_ui's egui "x" button, not the 8 pt rounded Varos card of the v8 flyout. Rounding them
  needs a change in craft-ui (external git dependency) or Varos-drawn cards; not done. Owner
  should judge in the real window.
- First frame of a brand-new card is 360 pt tall, fitted on the next frame (one-frame jump).
- Fitting follows the contents once per contents size; a panel whose contents keep changing
  height (sections opening) re-fits; a card the user resized never re-fits.
- Dropping a card on the icon column closes it (upstream would pop it out of its icon).
- No UI switch for keep_open (as in lane-ux1).
