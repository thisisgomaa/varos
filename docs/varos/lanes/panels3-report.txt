lane-panels3 report — 2026-10-20 — implementer: Opus 5.5 — branch lane-panels3, base main a0221122. Not committed.

Owner feedback on panels2 cards: "they feel stuck"; grouped panels forget their group when closed;
they move only from a small grip on the left; they should open beside their own icon; while moving
only a blue outline follows the mouse and the card jumps on release. All of these came from
upstream's floating window (craft_ui). The cards are now drawn and moved by Varos.

WHAT THE PERSON WILL SEE
- A card moves from anywhere on its header (tab, empty header), live under the pointer, every
  frame. No outline, no jump on release. Escape while holding it puts it back.
- Resize from the bottom-right corner, the right edge or the bottom edge, also live.
- While held, a card stays left of the dock and above the status bars (the pointer may go over
  the dock to dock it).
- A new card opens beside its icon: its top level with the icon, its right edge 6 pt left of the
  dock. If something is in the way it goes under the cards in its way; if there is no room under
  them, right above them; if there is no room there either, it cascades (24 pt steps). A card
  never goes below the bottom of the icon column (no longer over the status bars).
- Grouping: drop a card on another card's header and it joins as tabs (the target header lights
  up while you hold it there). Drag a tab out of a card with several tabs and it becomes its own
  card that follows the pointer at once. Drag a tab along its own header to reorder the tabs, live.
  Drop a card on the icon column or the dock and it docks (an icon panel goes back to its icon).
- Group memory: close a card with several tabs by its × and open any of its panels again: the
  whole card comes back with the same tabs, the same tab in front, the same place and size (also
  after a restart; saved in ui.json varos_panel_stack.groups; old profiles load with none).
  A tab closed alone by its icon opens again in its card, at its place among the tabs, in front.
  A dock tab grouped into a card (e.g. Character + Layers) goes back to the dock when the card
  closes and comes back into the card when it reopens.
- Look (kit, panel-kit.html section 5): 8 pt corners, 1 pt line (theme border token), no shadow,
  32 pt header with the tabs on the left and x then the panel menu on the right. Tab in front: dark
  well (input token) with a 1 pt line and bright text, no azure. NOTE: the mockup's .tab.on uses
  the surface colour; the brief said "dark well like the kit", so I used the well. One-line change
  in panel_stack::card if the owner prefers the mockup.
- Everything from panels2 is kept: cards never over the rail/dock, sizes bounded by the room,
  places remembered, Tab/Shift+Tab hide and bring back, shortcuts toggle, an agent's window.panel
  only shows, workspace restore re-enrols cards, Auto-Collapse transition, fit to contents.

HOW
- Cards are still upstream floating groups (model, ui.json, docking commands, drop targets), but
  panel_docking::show no longer gives them to craft_ui's DockArea (panel_stack::owns filter);
  panel_stack::draw paints each card in an egui Area with the same id upstream used, and
  panel_stack::track moves/resizes it by writing the group's rect before it is drawn (same frame).
- Drops go through upstream: a card header -> window.panel.float {group, onto}; dock/icon column/
  dock column -> window.panel.dock (via floating::drop_target); tab reorder and tab placement ->
  window.panel.layout Move.
- Upstream bodies, panel menu (panels::panel_menu) and commands are reused unchanged; craft_ui is
  not modified.

LEFT / RISKS
- A brand-new egui area is drawn once invisibly to measure itself: a card that has just been torn
  out (and the rest of its card when its first tab was torn out) is invisible for one frame
  (~8-16 ms). The first open of a card is also invisible for that frame (Varos uses it to fit and
  place the card at its real size, so it appears once, in place). egui offers no public way to
  pre-seed an area's size; not fixable without changing egui.
- Upstream's dock tabs and rail icons dragged out still use upstream's outline preview (only cards
  were in scope); dropping them on a card's header joins the card.
- Drops between a card and native dock-column sets use upstream's drop targets; "above/below a
  card" (upstream set stacking) is ignored for cards (the card stays where let go).
- No reorder by keyboard; no animation on reorder (instant, as the toggle rule).
- Not hand-tested in the real window (owner hand test needed for feel: drag smoothness, cursor on
  the header is the normal arrow, resize cursors on the grips).
