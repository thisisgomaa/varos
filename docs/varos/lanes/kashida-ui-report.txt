WHAT THE USER SEES
- Paragraph panel, under the alignment buttons, for a paragraph that runs right to left (Arabic,
  Hebrew, or Right-to-Left direction): "Kashida  [Off | Auto | Force]" and "Strength" with a
  0-100 slider. Hidden for left-to-right paragraphs, where kashida does nothing.
  Azure fills only the active segment and the slider's track; 3 px outer corners; 1 px border;
  no shadow. The slider is greyed while Kashida is Off. The labels are provisional English.
- Auto is the default, at strength 50, which is exactly the old fixed behaviour. Off: word
  spacing only. Force: deeper (more tatweels per word and more of each line's slack), and the
  last line of a "justify, last line aligned" paragraph takes kashida too. That line keeps its
  spaces natural, so it stays short of the measure.
- A click on a segment is one undo step. A slider drag previews live and is one undo step on
  release. Paragraph panel > Reset Panel sets it back to Auto 50. Paragraph styles capture it.
- Fonts that HarfRust shapes with morx now take kashida automatically: Geeza Pro, Al Nile,
  Baghdad, Damascus, KufiStandardGK, DecoType Naskh, Farah, Diwan Kufi, Al Bayan, Nadeem. All 10
  were measured on this Mac. Before this, none of them took kashida.

STRENGTH MAPPING (crates/text-arabic/src/lib.rs, policy())
  strength 0..=24   1 per word, share of slack 0.25 + 0.35*s/50
  strength 25..=50  2 per word, same formula (0.60 at 50 = Balanced)
  strength 51..=75  3 per word, 0.60 + 0.40*(s-50)/50
  strength 76..=100 4 per word, same (1.00 at 100 = Display)
  0 = varos-text Minimal, 50 = Balanced, 100 = Display.
  Force adds 1 per word (max 5) and +0.40 share (max 1.0).
  plan_with() is varos-text's plan() with a numeric policy. A test shows it equals plan() slot
  for slot at Balanced and Minimal.
  varos-text itself is pinned and unchanged.

MORX PATH (crates/text/src/kashida.rs)
- HarfRust applies morx/mort for horizontal text whenever the font has it, and ignores GSUB. Its
  morx shaping never sets the safe-to-insert-tatweel flag. morx_shaped(face) means the face has
  morx/mort and its letters join (ببب is not 3 x isolated ب). A morx face whose letters don't
  join takes no kashida.
- Candidate slots come from varos-text's candidates(): joining_allows between two joining
  letters; never inside لا (lam then alef is not allowed); never next to harakat; never after a
  right-joining letter.
- Each trial re-shape (same face, same HarfRust) is kept only if:
  (a) the glyph count grows by exactly one per tatweel, each tatweel is its own zero-length U+0640
      glyph, and the tatweels sit at the requested slots;
  (b) every authored glyph is unchanged (gid, cluster byte and length, dx and dy), so the
      neighbours' joining forms and all marks survive;
  (c) no .notdef;
  (d) the word grew by the tatweels' advances, within 1 font unit each.
  A failed check rejects the trial and the plan moves to the next slot.
- Damascus has GSUB (arab) and morx. HarfRust uses morx, so it is on this path.
  NOTE for the font-menu lane: its "AAT" marker uses morx && no GSUB arab, so it does not mark
  Damascus. To match HarfRust it should be "has morx/mort".

SYNTHETIC MORX FACE
write-fonts writes no AAT tables, and adding an empty morx to Noto makes HarfRust stop joining.
So the positive morx path is tested two ways:
- on Noto forced onto the path by a test-only override (FORCED_MORX), with HarfRust's flags
  ignored: same result as with its flags, [1296, 648, 0, 0];
- on the installed morx fonts, guarded.
The empty-morx Noto is the negative test: it doesn't join, so it gets no kashida.
