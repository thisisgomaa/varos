  • Row 32, control 24. Every row in every panel is 32 tall; fields, dropdowns, toggles and tracks are 24 inside it.
  • Padding 10 / gap 8 / 4. 10 px from the card edge; 8 px between controls on a row; 4 px between a glyph and its box.
  • Two columns. Number rows hold two controls of equal width (115 px in a 260 px card), so glyphs line up down the panel.
  • Glyph first. Every field has a 16 px glyph on its left that says what it is; the tooltip carries the words.
  • Anatomy. glyph · stepper ▲▼ · value · preset caret. Steppers on numbers only; caret only where presets exist.
  • Sections. 1 px full-bleed divider with 10 px air; foldable sections get a 10 px caps title and a chevron.
  • Toggles light up. ON = dark well + bright icon + hairline. Never an azure bar or underline.
  • Azure is a scalpel. Only a focused field border and the current swatch ring use it.
  • Mixed and disabled. Mixed reads "–" and lights nothing; disabled is 40% and keeps its value.
  • Cards. 8 px corners, 1 px line, no shadow, 32 px header; controls 3 px corners.

What changed from the approved version

  • Rows 24 → 32, fields 20 → 24.
  • Every indent and spacing field gets a glyph and a stepper (no caret: no presets).
  • Group heads become the kit's section title; dividers run edge to edge.
  • Indents and spacing are no longer greyed; first-line indent sits alone on its row, as in Illustrator.
  • Unchanged: order, icons, lit wells, the dashed "Auto picked this" ring, Kashida Off/Auto/Force + strength.

Rail rules

  • Rail 40 wide, buttons 32 × 32, glyphs drawn on a 24 grid, stroke 1.6.
  • Groups are split by a thin double-line grip (16 px wide, 12 tall); drag the grip to move the group.
  • Lit (dark well, bright glyph) = that card is open. Hover = grey tint.
  • Tooltip = name + Illustrator shortcut, after a short hover; no labels on the rail itself.
